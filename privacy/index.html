Privacy

This page explains what the Get started form collects and how FirstRing TX handles calls and texts about that request. Questions: call or text (817) 719-6804 or email hello@firstringtx.com.

What you can send us

If you use the form on firstringtx.com, you can send:

  • Business name, your name, phone number, and email
  • Trade: HVAC, plumbing, roofing, electrical, cleaning, or other
  • Service area, crew size, and about how many calls you get in a month
  • How calls are answered now
  • What you want help with, and the problems getting in the way
  • Which plan you want to talk about: Starter, Growth, Pro, or not sure
  • The best time for a call, and English or Spanish
  • Whether you agree to receive calls and texts

How we use it

FirstRing TX uses this to call you back about missed-call text-back, 24/7 online booking, and automatic review requests for your shop. Anthony is the local FirstRing rep at the phone number and email above. Sending the form is a request for a call back. It does not by itself start a paid plan.

Calls and texts

If you check the consent box, you agree that FirstRing TX may call and text the number you entered about your request and about FirstRing.

  • Message frequency varies.
  • Message and data rates may apply.
  • Reply STOP to opt out of texts.
  • Reply HELP for help.
  • Consent is not a condition of purchase. You can ask us to call you without agreeing to texts.

We do not share your mobile number with third parties for their own marketing.

Carriers are not liable for delayed or undelivered messages.

The rest of the site

Prices, the month-to-month plans, and the 30-day refund of the first month are described on firstringtx.com. This page does not change those descriptions. See also the Terms.
